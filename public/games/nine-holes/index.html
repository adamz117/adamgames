How to play
  1. The line sweeps side to side. Tap the hole to move it, then Lock the line.
  2. The needle climbs the power track. Swing inside the green band: it marks the power that reaches the pin, or the pure strike when the pin is out of reach.
  3. Finish on the green: inside the gold ring is one putt, the pale ring two, anywhere else three.
